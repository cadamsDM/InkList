About THE INKLIST

Your comprehensive guide to Christchurch's vibrant tattoo scene.

Our Story

THE INKLIST was born from a passion for tattoo art and a desire to connect talented artists with those seeking their perfect piece. Founded in Christchurch in 2024, we recognized the need for a centralized resource to showcase the diverse range of tattoo studios and artists in our city.

This small project was fueled by a need to connect google searches, to instragram, website, and facebook pages as comprehensive directory for tattoo enthusiasts in Christchurch. We're proud to support local artists big or small and help people discover the incredible talent that our city has to offer.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to connect people with their ideal tattoo experience. We believe that finding the right artist is just as important as the design itself, and we're committed to making that process as seamless as possible.

We strive to provide accurate, up-to-date information about each studio, including their specialties, pricing, and location. Whether you're getting your first tattoo or adding to your collection, we're here to help you find the perfect match.

How It Works

Our directory allows you to browse through Christchurch's tattoo studios, filter by style and price range, and explore artist portfolios. Each listing provides detailed information about the studio's atmosphere, hygiene standards, and booking process.

We regularly update our listings to ensure you have the most current information, and we're always adding new studios and artists to our growing community.
